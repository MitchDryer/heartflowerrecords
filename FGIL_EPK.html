finding ground in love

Mitch Zachry


finding ground in love is a song by Mitch Zachry to be released on the 26th of June through Heartflower Records. It is the first single from an upcoming EP called deep blue love i'm coming home to bed! The song is about falling into the depths of life to find inner peace.

Mitch Zachry is a songwriter, guitarist, and producer from Aotearoa/New Zealand, now based in Melbourne, Australia. Over the past two years he has released two self-produced albums and performed throughout Australia and New Zealand, developing a sound that blends existential songwriting, expressive guitar playing, and atmospheric production.
